# solis-content

Learning & teaching content repository — originally hosting structured (JSON) curriculum content, now extended with a **free-download mirror of Teachers Arena (arena.co.ke)** materials as PDF/DOC documents.

## Repository structure

| Folder | Contents |
|---|---|
| `revision/` | **KCSE** 2005–2017 (171) · **KCPE** 2000–2017 (137) · **KPSEA** Grade 6 (47) · **KJSEA** Grade 9 (25) · KCPE 2022 with marking schemes (13) — organized by exam type → year |
| `exams/` | Form 1–4 exams & marking schemes (by Form) · KNEC SBA assessments (Grades 3–9) · KNEC primary tests with marking schemes · Holiday/homework assignments |
| `lesson-notes/` | Primary notes (Class 4–8) · CBC notes (Grade 1–6) · Junior Secondary incl. JSS teacher training · Secondary notes Form 1–4 by subject (Mathematics, Biology, Chemistry, Physics, English, Kiswahili, Computer Studies, Business Studies, CRE, History, Geography, Agriculture, Home Science, Literature/set books, Science, Social Studies) |
| `schemes-of-work/` | CBC schemes of work Grades 1–6 — all subjects, Terms 1–3 |
| `curriculum-designs/` | CBC curriculum designs Grades 1–9 + subject syllabi + combined-subject designs |
| `lesson-plans/` | CBC lesson plans PP1–PP2 & Grades 1–6 — all subjects |
| `forms/` | Professional forms: TSC / KNEC exams / MOE schools / scholarships & loans / other |
| `professional-docs/` | CBC resources (report books PP1–G6 & SNE, assessment rubrics, IEP reports, lesson plan templates, record of work, teacher professionalism) · KNEC briefing notes |
| `manifest.json` | Index of native JSON content + `pdfMaterials` summary of the pushed document mirror |

## Notes
- Documents are PDF unless noted (a few `.doc`/`.docx` as published on the source site).
- 4 source files are unavailable on arena.co.ke itself (dead links / Drive-restricted) and are listed in `manifest.json → pdfMaterials.unavailableFiles`.
